Zombie Shorts Uploader

Zombie Shorts Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the animated YouTube series 30 днів у місті зомбі (@30DnivZombie) and 30 Days in Zombie City (@30DaysZombieCity).

It runs on the owner's own computer and, with the owner's permission, uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API to:

  • upload the owner's own episodes and schedule their publication;
  • add them to the series playlists and set their thumbnails;
  • post a comment under the owner's own videos;
  • read view and audience-retention statistics of the owner's own videos.

It is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's account.
